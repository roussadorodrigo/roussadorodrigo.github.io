About me

I'm a student in a Portuguese higher-education program that combines embedded systems, control engineering and project and financial management. I like the places where those meet: a sensor reading that has to become a stable motor output, or a bootloader that has to survive a firmware update.

I learn by building and debugging on real boards. I work in small, testable steps, read the driver source when a call blocks, and prefer a minimal fix over a rewrite. Alongside the hardware work I write technical reports and financial analyses, so I'm comfortable explaining a result as well as producing it.

I work in both Portuguese and English.
